FAQ

Good questions

Everything you need to know about ordering from Pizzarazzi MNL.

How far in advance should I order?

We operate on a commission basis, so please order at least 1 day (24 hours) in advance. This lets us prepare fresh dough and source the best ingredients.

What size are your pizzas?

All our pizzas are 12 inches — perfect for 2–3 people. Authentic Neapolitan-style with a light, airy crust.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash on delivery or pickup, GCash, Maya and bank transfer. We'll send payment details when we confirm your order.

Do you deliver?

Yes. We offer takeaway and delivery within Marikina City and nearby areas. Delivery fees may apply depending on your location.

How do I reheat my pizza?

Microwave: 20–45 seconds on high.
Convection oven: 5–8 minutes at 150–200 °C until the cheese melts. Don't burn it!

What makes your pizza special?

Authentic Neapolitan preparation: a starter fermented for 12 hours, dough that rises for 12+ hours, fresh sauce from crushed tomatoes and herbs, and generous toppings.

How do I know my order went through?

After you place an order you'll see a confirmation with your order reference. We'll reply by email, text or call to confirm your slot and payment.
